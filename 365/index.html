Why Do We Have Leap Years?

It takes Earth approximately 365 days and 6 hours (365.2422 days) to complete its orbit around the Sun.

Since a standard calendar year is exactly 365 days, those extra ~6 hours accumulate over 4 years into a full 24-hour day (6 × 4 = 24 hours).

Added Day: February 29th (366 total days).

*Century rule: Years divisible by 100 are NOT leap years unless they are also divisible by 400 (e.g., 2000 was a leap year, but 1900 was not).
